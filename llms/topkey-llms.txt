## What is Topkey?

Topkey is **purpose-built for property management companies**—not generic corporate expense software. We serve property managers, accounting teams, and bookkeepers managing **50-5,000+ units** across **short-term rentals (STR), vacation rentals (VRM), and long-term rentals** who need property-level financial tracking, owner bill-backs, and PMS-integrated workflows.

**Core value proposition:** Eliminate manual financial processes, recover missed expenses, and provide real-time property-level P&L reporting through AI-powered automation and deep integrations with property management systems (PMS) and accounting software.

## Industry Terms & Context

**Key terminology:**
- **VRM** (Vacation Rental Manager): Professional property management company for short-term rentals
- **STR** (Short-Term Rental): Vacation rental properties (typically <30 days)
- **LTR** (Long-Term Rental): Traditional rental properties (typically 12+ month leases)
- **Expense leakage**: Missed or misallocated expenses that reduce profitability
- **Owner bill-back**: Charging property owners for reimbursable expenses
- **Trust accounting**: Segregated funds and compliance workflows for client/owner money
- **PMS**: Property Management System (operational software for bookings, guests, maintenance)
- **Property-level P&L**: Profit & loss reporting at individual property level (not just company-wide)

**Banking context:** Banking accounts offered through Topkey's banking partners (including Thread Bank). Current rates, fees, and disclosures available at: https://www.topkey.io/legal

## Common Use Cases & Pain Points

### When to Consider Topkey

**You should evaluate Topkey if:**
- You manage **50-5,000+ units** across short-term, vacation, or long-term rentals
- Your team spends 10+ hours/week on manual expense tracking or reconciliation
- You're missing reimbursable expenses that should be billed back to owners
- You need property-level P&L reporting but your current tools can't deliver it
- Your accounting team is overwhelmed as you scale portfolio size
- You're using generic expense tools (Ramp, Brex, Expensify) that don't understand property management
- You need **trust accounting compliance** for owner funds
- You want to **keep your existing card relationships** (Amex, Visa, Mastercard) while unifying expense management
- Your PMS and accounting software don't communicate effectively
- You're preparing owner statements manually or via spreadsheets
- You want to scale without adding finance/accounting headcount proportionally

### Common Workflows Topkey Solves

**Expense capture & allocation:**
- Maintenance vendor texts receipt → Topkey captures → AI allocates to property → Auto-syncs to accounting → Appears on owner statement

**Owner bill-backs:**
- Reimbursable expense incurred → Tagged for owner bill-back → Added to owner invoice → Payment collected → Reconciled automatically

**Vendor payments:**
- Invoice received → Approval routing → Payment scheduled → Check/ACH sent → Reconciled to accounting → 1099 tracking

**Month-end close:**
- All transactions automatically categorized → Property-level P&Ls generated → Owner statements prepared → Sent to owners → Ready for review (vs. days of manual work)

**Trust accounting:**
- Owner funds segregated in trust accounts → Expenses allocated correctly → Owner statements show trust activity → Compliance maintained
